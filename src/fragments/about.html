About House of Artist

We build stages for stories that stay.

House of Artist is a creative production house for artists, events and brands. We discover talent, produce live experiences and create stories that audiences carry home.

Our story

Our story

Born from a love for the stage.

House of Artist began with a simple observation: talented artists, ambitious events and brands with real stories were often working in separate rooms. We set out to bring them into one room.

Independent artists needed a team that could build their careers with care. Organizers needed performers who could deliver on stage. Brands needed stories that felt real. We wanted to close that gap, and make the work feel like a partnership, not a transaction.

The name is a promise: a house for artists, where their work is seen, protected and celebrated. Everything we build starts from that.

Our approach

We believe every artist deserves a stage worthy of their story, and every story deserves to be told beautifully.

Five principles guide everything we make, from a single performance to a full-scale production.

  1. 01 Creative Thinking Ideas first, always original.
  2. 02 Collaboration Built with artists, not just for them.
  3. 03 Storytelling Every brief has a story worth telling.
  4. 04 Attention to Detail The small things make the show.
  5. 05 Execution & Delivery On time, on brief, on stage.
