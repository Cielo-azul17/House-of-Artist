1.Who we are

House of Artist Entertainment LLP ("House of Artist", "we", "us") is the data fiduciary for the personal data described here. Our registered office is at [Registered office address, City, PIN], India. You can reach us at [hello@yourdomain.com].

This policy explains what we collect through this website and our work with artists, organizers and brands, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It is written to follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025, along with the Information Technology Act, 2000.

2.What we collect

We only collect what we need to answer you and do the work:

  • Details you give us — name, email, phone, your craft or brand, event type, date, city, budget range and anything you write in the message box.
  • Links you share — portfolio, showreel or social profiles you send so we can see your work.
  • Project records — contracts, invoices, payment references and correspondence once we start working together.
  • Technical data — IP address, device and browser type, pages visited and referring links, collected by our hosting and analytics tools.
  • Media — photographs and video recorded at events we produce, which may include attendees.

3.How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and understand whether we are a fit.
  • To plan, quote and deliver the event, campaign or representation you asked for.
  • To issue contracts, invoices and receipts, and to keep accounting records.
  • To share occasional updates about our artists and events, only if you asked for them.
  • To keep the website secure and understand which pages are useful.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

4.Consent and your choices

When you submit a form on this site, you consent to us using those details to respond and to proceed with the work you asked about. Some processing happens under the legitimate uses recognised by the DPDP Act, such as meeting a legal obligation.

You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to [hello@yourdomain.com]. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent, though it may mean we can no longer continue an ongoing engagement.

5.Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only where the work requires it:

  • Artists, managers or clients directly involved in your enquiry or event.
  • Vendors we engage for you — venues, production, sound and lighting, travel and hospitality.
  • Service providers who run our website, email, forms, storage and analytics, under contract.
  • Our accountants and legal advisors.
  • Government or law-enforcement authorities where we are legally required to disclose.

6.How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are kept for [24 months] and then deleted. Records tied to contracts, invoices and taxes are kept for as long as Indian law requires, currently [8 years] for financial records. Event photographs and footage are kept in our archive unless you ask us to remove images of you.

7.How we protect it

We use access controls, encrypted connections, password management and limited staff access. We review the vendors who handle data on our behalf. No system is perfect, but if a personal data breach affects you we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as the DPDP Rules require.

8.Children’s data

This website and our services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data of anyone under 18 without verifiable parental consent, and we do not track or target children or show them advertising. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

9.Your rights

As a data principal under the DPDP Act, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data of yours we hold and how we have used it.
  • Correct, complete or update anything inaccurate.
  • Erase your data where we no longer need it for the purpose or by law.
  • Nominate someone to exercise your rights if you cannot.
  • Raise a grievance about how we have handled your data.

Write to [hello@yourdomain.com] from the email address you used with us. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we will respond within [30 days].

10.Cookies and analytics

This site uses a small number of cookies: ones that make the site work, and analytics cookies that tell us which pages are visited. Analytics cookies are only set if you accept them. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the site may then behave differently.

11.Embedded video and third parties

Showreels and project videos on this site are hosted on YouTube and Vimeo and load only when you press play. At that point, the video provider may set its own cookies and receive your IP address under its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

12.Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and change the "last updated" date at the top. If the change is significant, we will tell the people it affects.

13.Grievance redressal

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, contact our grievance officer. If you are still not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
